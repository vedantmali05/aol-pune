# Happiness Course Repeated WhatsApp Messages

Quick reference repository of standard WhatsApp broadcast messages for Happiness Program organizers.

## 1. Welcome & Guidelines Message
```
✨ *Welcome to The Art of Living HAPPINESS PROGRAM!* ✨

Jai Gurudev! 🙏

So happy to have you join our family! 🌸

Just a few quick tips to make the most of these 4 days:

- Wear loose, comfortable clothes so you can sit and move easily 🧘‍♂️
- Bring your water bottle and keep sipping water throughout 💧
- Keep your phone on silent to stay present in the room 🔕
- Reach the venue a few minutes early ⏰
- Don’t forget to bring your biggest smile! 😊

See you there! 🌿✨
```

## 3. Day 1 Homework & Guidelines Message
```
✨ *DAY 1 Homework* ✨

Jai Gurudev! 🙏

1. Shift from *"Why Why"* to *"Wow"* in every situation 😀
2. Practice *Ujjayi breaths* 😤
3. Practice *Three-Stage Pranayama*:
- Stage 1 (8 breaths)
- Stage 2 (8 breaths)
- Stage 3 (6 breaths)
- Followed by 3 rounds of *Bhastrika*
- Then sit quietly and relax ☺️

*Reflect on 2 Questions:* 📝
- When will you be happy?
- What do you need to be happy?

*Lifestyle Guidelines:*
- Drink lots of water today 💧
- Take sattvik food
- Strictly avoid smoking 🤒, alcohol 🍷, and tobacco

See you all tomorrow! 🌸🧘‍♂️🌿
```
